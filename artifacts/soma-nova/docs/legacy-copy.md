### **Hi! I’m Emily Schofield CPT/CNC**

Why Protea Fitness? The protea flower (pro-tee-ah) symbolizes transformation and courage. I believe that anyone has the ability transform their health with caring guidance, and that those who do so have great courage. Consider me your guide as you walk the path to fitness.

I’m certified through the National Academy of Sports Medicine both in personal training, and in nutrition coaching, and am constantly learning the latest in wellness. This work is my passion, especially when a client is interested in their long term health. While I can help you improve your appearance, my heart is in helping you make life style changes for a better quality of living!

I’m a busy mom with a chronic illness, I really do understand how hard it can be to take charge of your health. I will never judge you, push you to injury, or shame you, but I will _always_ draw out your personal best. My job is to guide you safely toward a purpose, not take your money for endless sessions. Let’s work together to lose that weight, gain more strength, or pursue whatever your goals may be. I know you can do it. Let’s get started!

## **Focused on You**

### Corrective Training

Repetitive movement means overworked, imbalanced muscles (think carpal tunnel or chronic neck tightness.) I will help you get those muscles evened out with personalized corrective exercises.

### Bodyweight Training

From mat work and Pilates-based poses to high intensity cardio, we can exercise anywhere with what we have!

### Strength/resistance Training

Often overlooked in women and older people, using weights and resistance bands are an important part of everyone’s health routine! I will teach you how to use equipment safely.

### Special Circumstances Training

I often work with seniors, youths, post-physical therapy patients, and people with chronic illnesses. I’m well acquainted with physical limitations, and am always up for the challenge to modify your program to be safe and enjoyable!

### Nutrition Coaching

As a certified nutrition coach I can help you navigate past fad diets and nutrition myths so you can make educated nutrition choices to support your goals.
